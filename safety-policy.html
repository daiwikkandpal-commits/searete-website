Safety Policy

Safety, from the bridge to the boardroom

Searete India IFSC Private Limited follows the safety policies expected of a responsible organisation — extending beyond crew and cargo to safeguarding the environment, through regular review and improvement of our standards. Our consistent, disciplined approach to safety and environmental protection has helped us reach a high standard of operational safety.

We understand that strong Quality Management, Health and Safety practices, and CSR efforts around environmental protection are what drive long-term business success.

How We Deliver It

Four pillars of our safety programme

Stakeholders

Building strong, mutually beneficial relationships with customers, service providers and employees.

Compliance

Complying with International Conventions on Safety of Life at Sea, Pollution Prevention, and seafarer Training & Watchkeeping.

Cyber & OT

Protecting ship automation and control systems through OT networks, detecting and addressing cyber and operational risks.

Seaworthiness

Systematic checks on inventory so critical spare parts are always available on board, at all times.
